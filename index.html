How this works

We estimate the cost of your current spending basket one year earlier using the September 2026 annual inflation rates for the 12 NCPI divisions, then calculate the percentage increase to the current basket. Your household expenditure shares determine your personal weights. This is an approximation: a rigorous fixed-basket comparison would use previous-period spending weights and matched price relatives. The national rate of 5.4% uses official national expenditure weights and is shown for comparison.

*The estimated annual price impact is the difference between the current monthly basket and its estimated cost one year earlier, holding quantities fixed; it is not an additional monthly bill. Exact previous-year equivalent cost, assuming fixed quantities and the same category-level price changes, is calculated as the sum of current category spending divided by (1 + category inflation/100). Individual items may experience very different price changes.
