How are the estimates calculated?

For each division, estimated cost one year ago = current spending ÷ (1 + division annual inflation rate ÷ 100). Personal inflation = (total current spending ÷ total estimated year-ago cost − 1) × 100. The estimated monthly price difference is current spending minus estimated year-ago spending.

This is an illustrative fixed-purchases comparison using broad NCPI division averages. Your actual prices, purchases and inflation experience may differ. The estimated monthly price difference is not a new charge or the change in your actual spending.

💡 Practical money tip

Compare prices where practical, prioritise essentials and review recurring expenses. Avoid cutting necessary healthcare or other essential spending.
