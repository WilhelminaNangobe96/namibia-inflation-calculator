What drove inflation?

Transport, food and non-alcoholic beverages, and housing and utilities were the three largest contributors. Their contributions, displayed to one decimal place, total 4.5 percentage points of the 5.6% annual inflation rate. The other divisions account for the remainder.

Your spending, your experience

Enter your monthly spending

Enter your approximate current monthly spending in Namibian dollars for each of the 12 Namibia Consumer Price Index (NCPI) categories. Leave any category at zero if you do not spend on it.

You can calculate personal inflation without entering income. Enter income if you also want to see how much of it goes towards the expenses you enter and what may remain.

A CPI basket weight shows how much a category counts in the national spending basket. A higher weight generally means it has more influence on national inflation. This is different from the share of your own spending.

General advice for managing rising prices

Rising prices can put pressure on household budgets. Prioritise essential needs, distinguish necessary expenses from non-essential purchases, and reduce avoidable spending where possible. Compare prices, plan purchases, avoid unnecessary borrowing and review your budget regularly as prices and circumstances change. Aim to live within your means and save where possible.
